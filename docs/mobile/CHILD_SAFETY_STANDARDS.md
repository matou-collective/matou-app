# Child Safety Standards (Matou App)

**Effective date:** 2026-08-29
**Contact for safety issues:** `sysadmin@matou.nz`

These standards apply to the Matou App and every community that uses it.
They exist to meet app-store requirements for apps with user-generated
content and in-app communication (chat, notices, comments).

## Commitment

We do not allow child sexual exploitation or abuse in any form, and we
treat every report seriously.

## Prohibited

In chat, notices, profiles, comments or any other content:

- child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexual content involving
  minors;
- sexual solicitation of minors, grooming or exploitation;
- content encouraging self-harm, suicide or violence against minors;
- harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats or predatory behaviour;
- sharing a minor's (or anyone's) personal information without consent;
- content facilitating trafficking, abuse or exploitation;
- any illegal content involving minors.

## Reporting

Report immediately to `sysadmin@matou.nz` **and** to your community's
administrators. Include what happened and when, the channel or notice,
screenshots or message text if safe to provide, and the display names or
identifiers involved.

## Moderation and enforcement

Communities are administrator-moderated. Administrators can remove or
restrict content and channels, restrict chat participation, and suspend or
remove members. Because content is cryptographically signed, records of
who posted what can be preserved for investigation.

## Response

Reports involving minors, CSAM or immediate threats are prioritised. Where
we or a community become aware of possible CSAM or exploitation we will
restrict access to the content and accounts involved, preserve evidence as
the law requires, and report to the appropriate authorities where legally
required or appropriate.

## Age

The App is intended for adults (18+). Communities must not knowingly admit
members under that age.

## Data

Handling a safety report may involve processing limited personal data
(message text, identifiers) for safety and compliance purposes, as
described in the Privacy Policy.
