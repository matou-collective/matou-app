## Full description (4000)

```
Mātou is the app for members of the Mātou community. It is where members talk, keep up with notices, plan projects, and make decisions together, with the community's records held by the community rather than by a company.

Mātou is an Indigenous-led collective, and this app is built by Mātou for Mātou members. Identity in Mātou is relational: you become a member because people in the community recognise you, not because a company checked a document.

WHAT MEMBERS CAN DO
• Home: see who has joined, what is happening, and what needs your attention
• Chat: channels for kōrero (conversation) with other members
• Notices: announcements and updates from the collective
• Projects: implementation plans, milestones, and contributions, with budgets and sign-off
• Proposals: put decisions to the community and see where they stand
• Members: the member directory, and invitations for stewards

WHO THIS APP IS FOR
This app is for the Mātou community only. It is not a general platform for other groups or communities to run. You join by invitation or by request: set up a profile, the app creates your identity on your phone, and Mātou stewards approve you. There is no public sign-up. If you are not part of Mātou, the app will not be useful to you; to learn about the collective, visit matou.nz.

YOUR IDENTITY AND YOUR DATA
• Your identity is a cryptographic key created on your device, backed by a 12-word recovery phrase that only you hold. There is no password and no account with us.
• Community data is encrypted and synchronised peer to peer between members' devices and the community's own nodes.
• No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and nothing sold or shared with third parties.

Keep your recovery phrase safe. It is the only way to bring your identity to a new phone.

Privacy policy: https://matou.nz/privacy
Terms of use: https://matou.nz/terms
Questions: sysadmin@matou.nz
```

### Screenshots — how they are captured

Captured from the app itself, not from a device: the framing crops all native
chrome (no status bar, no clock, no notch), so a browser capture at the phone
raster is indistinguishable from a device one — and skips the `.aar`/APK build
and the emulator entirely.

The whole pipeline is reproducible from a clean test stack:

```sh
# 1. test infra + two backends (one identity per backend)
/clean-start test                       # keri + any-sync test networks
cd backend && make build
MATOU_ENV=test ./bin/server &                                   # steward :9080
MATOU_ENV=test MATOU_SERVER_PORT=9280 \
  MATOU_DATA_DIR=$PWD/data-test-storeshot-member ./bin/server &  # member :9280

# 2. org setup + member registration + approval (~3 min); also takes 06-pending
cd frontend
npx playwright test -c playwright.storeshots.config.ts --project=storeshots-bootstrap

# 3. seed believable content (idempotent — safe to re-run)
node ../docs/mobile/store-listing/scripts/seed-store-data.mjs

# 4. capture the remaining shots (~45 s; re-run freely while iterating)
npx playwright test -c playwright.storeshots.config.ts --project=storeshots-capture

# 5. frame them
cd ../docs/mobile/store-listing
python3 scripts/frame-screenshot.py raw/02-home.png screenshots/02-home.png \
  "See what is happening at a glance"
```

The community, cast and content all come from `cast.json` — change names there,
never in the captures. `raw/` holds the unframed captures; `screenshots/` holds
the framed assets that go to the store.

Rules: no real members' names or messages without their say-so; no
AI-generated imagery; screens in light theme; status bar clean. First two
screenshots matter most — they show in search results.
